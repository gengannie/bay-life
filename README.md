# Bay Life 🌉💻

**Live your Silicon Valley software engineer story.**

**Bay Life** is a browser-based, Sims-style life simulation game set in the San Francisco Bay Area, inspired by the game mechanics of [Lagos Life](https://lagoslife.app/) and crafted following **Apple Human Interface Guidelines** (macOS Sequoia / iOS 18 / visionOS frosted glass materials, SF Pro typography, Dynamic Island notifications, and fluid spatial UI).

---

## ✨ Core Game Mechanics

### 1. Engineer Creation & Bay Area Origins
- **3-Step Character Creator**: Customize your engineer's look (skin tone, hairstyle, signature Bay Area outfit from *Patagonia Down Vest* and *YC Startup Hoodie* to *Keynote Turtleneck* and *Arc'teryx Shell*, plus *AirPods Max* or *Apple Vision Pro*).
- **Traits & Life Aspirations**: Pick 2 engineer traits (*10x Flow State*, *Single-Origin Snob*, *Founder Reality Distortion*, *Competitive Programmer*, *Longevity Biohacker*, *TC-Obsessed Shitposter*) and pursue a Silicon Valley Aspiration (*Sand Hill Unicorn*, *L8 Principal Fellow*, *Silicon Valley Land Baron*, *Cerebral Valley Super-Connector*, or *100x Polymath Architect*).
- **4 Starting Origins**:
  - 🎓 **Bootstrapped New Grad** (Standard debt-free start in a Sunnyvale Hacker House)
  - 🦄 **Stanford Dropout (SAFE Note)** ($18k runway, +25% Startup MVP progress, $15k accelerator note)
  - ✈️ **Global 10x Grinder** (Starts with boosted Coding & System Design + vested RSUs in a Daly City In-Law)
  - 🥂 **Palo Alto Trust Fund Kid** (Starts in a SoMa Glass Loft with $55k cash, $25k RSUs, and a personal EV)

### 2. Interactive 2.5D Isometric Living & Venues
- **Isometric Apartment & Loft Grid**: Click any tile to walk or click any placed furniture object (*Eight Sleep Pod 4 Ultra*, *Pro Display XDR + Dual H100 Rig*, *La Marzocco Linea Micra Espresso Bar*, *39°F Cold Plunge & Cedar Sauna*, *Toto Neorest Smart Washlet*, *Apple Vision Pro Spatial Lounge*, *Tonal Home Gym*) to queue actions.
- **PG&E Grid Shutoffs & Backup Power** (*Lagos Life's NEPA mechanic, Bay Area edition*): Survive sudden PG&E Public Safety Power Shutoffs and Comcast outages by installing an **EcoFlow Delta Pro + Starlink** or **Tesla Powerwall 3**, or commute to a cafe/co-working space.
- **Action Queue & Autonomous Free Will**: Queue up to 5 actions with real-time progress bars, or let your engineer autonomously care for low needs when idle.

### 3. Career Ladders (L3 → L8), Work Tones & Startup Studio
- **4 Career Tracks**:
  - 🌈 **Goggle Cloud & AI** (`L3` → `L8 Principal Distinguished Fellow`)
  - 🧠 **OpenMind Frontier Lab** (`R1` → `R6 Chief Scientist`)
  - 🍎 **Fruit Co. Platform Architecture** (`ICT2` → `SVP of Software Engineering`)
  - 🌲 **Sequoia / Benchmark Canopy** (`Founding Engineer #1` → `Managing General Partner`)
- **Work Shifts & Focus Tones**: Choose how you work during shifts (*Deep Work*, *10x War Room Grind*, *Micro-Kitchen & Blind Gist*, *Align with Skip-Level VP*, or *Rest & Vest on Rooftop*) and navigate mid-shift PagerDuty SEV-1 dilemmas.
- **YC Startup Studio**: Build your own AI startup MVP, grow MRR and active users, and pitch Sand Hill Road VCs from *Weekend Prototype* to *Series A Unicorn*.

### 4. Bay Area World Map & Authentic Commutes
- Explore 9 iconic locations across **San Francisco**, **Peninsula**, and **South Bay**:
  - *The Plex Campus (Mountain View)*, *The Spaceship Park (Cupertino)*, *YC & Frontier AI Loft (SoMa)*, *Dolores Park & Tartine (Mission)*, *Hayes Valley & Blue Bottle Salon*, *Sand Hill Road VC Row (Menlo Park)*, *Dogpatch Boulders & Equinox*, *Ferry Building Marketplace*, and *Audio SF Rooftop Club*.
- Commute via **Caltrain Electric / BART**, **Free Tech Commuter Shuttle (G-Bus)**, **Waymo One Autonomous Jaguar**, **Uber Black**, or your own **Porsche Taycan / Model S Plaid**.

### 5. BayOS iPhone Suite
- **Levels.fyi**: Compare L3–L8 total compensation, track promo readiness, and switch companies.
- **Schwab Wealth**: Liquidate vested RSUs, invest in the Nasdaq/NVDA AI index, pay off SAFE notes, or buy an EV.
- **YC Studio**: Track startup valuation, MRR, and pitch VCs.
- **DoorDash & Instacart**: Order *Sweetgreen*, *Dumpling Time*, *Boba Guys*, or restock your home fridge.
- **Zillow Bay Area**: Lease or purchase deeds from a *Daly City Garage In-Law* up to a *Pacific Heights Golden Gate Victorian Flat*.
- **Blind & Direct Messages**: Read anonymous verified TC threads and network with Bay Area founders, Staff engineers, and VCs.
